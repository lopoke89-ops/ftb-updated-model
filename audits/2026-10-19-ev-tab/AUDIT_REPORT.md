# EV Bets end-to-end audit — October 19, 2026

The tab is suitable for basketball research and prospective engineering tests after the corrections in this release. It remains an **experimental conditional EV display**, not a demonstrated predictive or profitable service. Arithmetic correctness and verified historical data do not establish that its future probabilities are right.

This audit inspected the collectors, browser refresh, frozen models, source/history cutoffs, lineup/availability logic, filters, browser archive, grader and publication pipeline. It captured new public markets, re-extracted every final-box PDF, reproduced model diagnostics, traced representative estimates to their source PDFs, and exercised malformed/stale/failure cases. No numerical model was changed and no old research record was rewritten.

## Source and numerical verification

Fresh extraction of all **1,322 original NBA gamebooks** reconciled **28,716 played appearances** and **1,952,688 stat comparisons** with no differences. SQLite integrity was `ok`. Regular season (1,230), playoffs (85), play-in (6) and Cup final (1) remain separate. Three printed 00:00 appearances retain participation. DNPs are not counted as played zero-stat games. The PDF/opening ledgers and their existing hash-linked event evidence are preserved; this pass did not independently re-transcribe every play-by-play page.

The initial new FanDuel capture contained **2,330 offers**, including **139 First Team Basket offers**, across **16 fixtures / 48 source responses**. Independent current ESPN roster joins and source-envelope replay produced **zero differences** and 284 complete two-way pairs. Six issues were unposted Points/Threes/Quick Bets tabs on two fixtures, rather than fabricated missing prices. See `price-replay.json`. Exact overlapping milestones are never normalized as an exclusive outcome set.

A second, separately timestamped capture plus newly retrieved roster/depth/injury context traced **2,328 offers** through the updated service. It produced **494 Points**, **495 Threes** and **20 First Team Basket** supported estimates: **1,009 total**, including **618 non-positive offers** retained before display filters. All real contracts passed the stricter guard. The remaining offers were withheld with explicit reasons. No capture timestamps were replaced.

**40,240 distribution checks** verified probability sums, over/under/push consistency and monotone alternate-line ordering. Each EV reconciled to `P(win) × (decimal − 1) − P(loss)`. Integer totals retain push mass; milestones have their exact inclusive threshold. Break-even is a price, not a bookmaker truth estimate. Decisive win excludes pushes; the full EV retains them as refunded stake. First Team Basket's five named outcomes plus other-scorer residual sum to one. Game First Basket EV and combined-parlay EV remain unsupported.

The 1,009 new strict audit records all grade **pending**, with **zero invalid records** against the currently loaded verified history. Their games are future fixtures. This proves the pipeline accepts its own complete records; it does not prove outcome accuracy. Representative traces retain exact quotes, model features, source PDFs/hashes and minutes sensitivity in `service-trace.json`. These early engineering captures are outside the declared last-15-minute primary checkpoint.

## Basketball and probability assessment

Points includes separate two-point, three-point and free-throw contributions. Threes uses 3PA/3PM. Minutes/opportunity and shrunken G/F/C rates are included where the frozen model selected them. Correlation between attempts and makes is an eligibility feature, not proof of advantage. Points selected a 0.5 defensive-rate exponent; Threes selected zero positional exponents. A roster role is a proxy, not a measured defender assignment or a causal scheme adjustment.

Eligible bench players can enter Points/Threes. A projected start/bench change conflicting with recent role withholds the count estimate. Out/Doubtful/scenario exclusions cannot generate eligible estimates. Questionable and unresolved future-game availability remain provisional. The Parlay grade never supplies an EV probability.

First Team Basket uses exactly the shared Parlay team-view resolver. Five valid projections support a **conditional projected-lineup scenario**; fresh NBA confirmation takes precedence and is required for finalized picks. Each of the five still needs 20 same-team prior regular-season starts; the selected scorer needs three wins and five losses. Previous-team history is not silently transferred into an unsupported first-team probability. Probabilities assume the exact five starts and do not estimate starting/void probability. Actual-five mismatches retain ordinary contract outcomes but are excluded from accuracy scoring for that conditional five. Projected and confirmed cohorts stay separate.

Reproduced count diagnostics are unchanged:

| Points | 0.19038 | 0.19899 | 0.19228 | 0.963 / 0.073 |

These are already-inspected retrospective observations, not historical FanDuel-offer returns. Strata, calibration bins, fixture-cluster intervals and weekly sensitivity are retained in `model-diagnostics.json`. Different threshold/coverage samples cannot be compared as evidence of a filtering benefit. First Team Basket remains weaker than its start-history Brier baseline: **0.15934 versus 0.15912**, with intercept **0.207** outside its declared promotion limit. Prior-season transfer and historical announcement availability were not validated by final-box lineup diagnostics.

Large apparent edges persist despite correct quote identity and EV arithmetic. One trace, Keldon Johnson 20+ at +1700, has model win **24.21%** and conditional EV **+335.70%**. Its same-team historical tally is **13/82 (15.85%)**, which is descriptive evidence rather than a calibrated probability. Halving modeled opportunity changes the estimated win chance to **1.76%** and EV to **−68.26%**. That is a diagnostic scenario, not a claim that his minutes should be halved. It demonstrates the sensitivity of these apparent edges to role/opportunity assumptions. No cap, bookmaker blending or post-diagnostic fit was applied to hide the disagreement.

## Refresh, records and settlement

The collector and browser schedule five-minute checks. Successful complete source responses remove disappeared markets; failures retain original times and cached/error state. Partial fixture failures are isolated. Stale, future-dated, cached, suspended, started, mistimed and unsupported contracts do not reach probabilities. Separate timers invalidate prices, confirmations, expected lineups, depth charts and injury evidence; saving rechecks applicable evidence at click time. Provider outages, rate limits, GitHub scheduling/startup/CDN/API behavior and device time still prevent a five-minute SLA.

Schema-4 captures retain negative offers and all-withheld states, complete context and service/model hashes. Changing a forecast, source, lineup or exclusion appends evidence; earlier records stay unchanged. Browser-local records are editable, depend on the view being open and can be cleared. The price branch's Git history helps source provenance but is not an independent probability/display issuance witness. A shared pre-tip issuer and independent fixture ledger remain required for a verifiable track record.

Grading supports exact milestones, half lines, integer pushes, DNP voids, played-zero rows, First Team Basket nonstarter voids and PDF first points including free throws. Missing outcomes remain pending; inconsistent/partial/conflicting final sources require review. Ordinary book outcomes and conditional-model accuracy eligibility remain distinct. Actual account settlement, venue/postponement/suspension exceptions, promotional protection and actual customer ROI are not verified.
