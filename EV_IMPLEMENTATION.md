# Experimental FanDuel EV Bets

Choose **2026–27 → Upcoming game → October 20, 2026**, with All opponents, the full statistical period, and regular-season/all competition. Open **EV Bets**. Prices are FanDuel Tennessee, for full-game Points and Made Threes. The existing Parlay grades, equal thirds, positive-correlation eligibility rule, season-average targets, first-score definitions and frozen research protocol are unchanged.

## Prices and refresh

`scripts/fanduel_odds.py` reads the public application configuration and Tennessee NBA competition listing, joins both team names and a unique nearby tipoff to ESPN fixtures, and collects the Points and Threes tabs. The application identifier is read from the site's public bundle, never from an account; it is not printed or embedded in exported records. No account login, betslip, wagering or location-spoofing operation is used.

Current ESPN matchup rosters resolve player names to canonical IDs. An optional Jr/Sr/II/III/IV suffix may be ignored only if the result remains unique across both rosters. FanDuel selection IDs are not treated as ESPN player IDs. A fully loaded tab is required; unposted tabs are recorded as unavailable. Quarter, half, combo, first-basket, and unsupported market types are excluded. Milestones take their threshold from the market; the runner's zero handicap is not the milestone. Over/under side, target, American/decimal consistency, player, fixture, period, and market status are validated independently.

The `nba-live-odds` branch retains normalized quotes, source-response hashes and source capture times. Each successful publication is a Git snapshot. The last capture of a failed market remains marked cached, with its original time. One failed fixture does not remove other fixtures. Markets missing from a successfully loaded tab do not retain earlier active prices. The collector covers the next three calendar days plus today, regular season only.

The workflow schedules five-minute checks. An active worker continues while upcoming regular-season fixtures are available, up to four hours, including advance prices before game day. Polls are anchored five minutes apart rather than adding request duration to every cycle. GitHub concurrency retains a pending successor. Scheduled startup is best effort; it is not a five-minute service guarantee. Source configuration/schema changes fail explicitly. Temporary network/5xx/429 failures get bounded retries; extended Retry-After responses defer collection. Earlier published data is not re-timestamped on failure.

The EV view independently checks the published feed every five minutes, skips hidden polling and refreshes when visible again. A quote must be at most five minutes old, active/open, from the correct Tennessee fixture and period, and before the earlier of the ESPN and FanDuel tipoffs. A separate expiry/tipoff timer removes expired rows without waiting for another collection; manual evidence saving rechecks validity at the action time. Cached, future, stale, suspended, mismatched, and started-game quotes do not generate EV estimates. Last request, last successful read, collector check, and quote capture time are distinct.

Each browser read first resolves the latest `nba-live-odds` commit through GitHub's public reference API, then fetches that immutable commit's `odds.json`. This avoids an older branch-file CDN copy even when a new snapshot has been published. The resolved commit and URL are retained in exported transport evidence. The reference must name the expected branch and a valid commit SHA. A version lookup, rate-limit, or file failure retains the previous snapshot with its original timestamps and exposes the error; it never renews an old quote's age. Public API and scheduled-worker availability still limit refresh reliability.

## Probability model

`ev_probability.js` is a separate experimental count-distribution engine. It uses complete PDF-verified prior appearances only, a 20-appearance minimum, recent minutes/attempts shrunk toward player history, separate two-point/three-point/free-throw conversion, and optional shrunken opponent/own G/F/C scoring rates per player-minute. A minimum ten-game team sample and 80% known-role minute coverage are required. Full scoring identities are checked. Positive FGA/FGM or 3PA/3PM correlation remains required. EV requires 20 prior appearances with the current team. Other-team history remains available in the original Parlay view, but is excluded from this EV model until current-team support is sufficient. Player names and present-day lineups cannot retroactively alter historical positions.

Development chooses mean form, global/player-specific dispersion blending and positional-rate exponents by likelihood using dates through Dec 15, 2025. CDF calibration uses Dec 16–Feb 15. Diagnostics use Feb 16–Apr 12, 2026. All predictions for an entire date are made before adding any of that date's outcomes. A positive-slope CDF calibrator preserves alternate-line ordering. This was an already inspected, corrected archive; these are later-date diagnostics, not a newly unseen live test. Probabilities are conditional on participation. Historical pregame injuries/announcements and prices were unavailable and are not reconstructed from final boxes.

The chosen Points model uses a 0.5 opponent-rate exponent and zero own-team exponent. Threes chose zero positional exponents. We do not force an unhelpful positional multiplier into the probability model. This does not change the original Parlay weighting. Projected role changes are withheld; no unvalidated injury/teammate-absence minutes multiplier is invented. Exact-line outcomes need five prior wins and five losses, and the target must stay within the calibration grid. These are operational support safeguards, not proof of positive EV. Questionable and unconfirmed availability remain provisional; Out/Doubtful/scenario exclusions cannot enter EV. Prior-season transfer is explicitly unvalidated. Postseason, preseason, first-basket and first-team-basket probability models are not provided by this release.

The model is fitted with `node scripts/train_ev.cjs`. Its engine, trainer, protocol, and archive hashes are checked before publication. The browser model and JSON must agree. Current-season verified history can update input features; it does not silently refit the frozen calibration parameters. A changed archive requires an explicit diagnostic rebuild.

## EV and research records

For $1 staked, expected profit is `P(win) * (decimal odds - 1) - P(loss)`. Pushes/voids return stake. For an integer over 3, winning requires at least 4 and exactly 3 pushes. For 3+ or over 2.5, winning requires at least 3. Bookmaker implied probability is a break-even price, not a model probability. The payload field called `trueOdds` contains decimal/fractional price formats, not an independently known true chance.

All supported offers for qualified players are evaluated, not just each team's existing top pick. Default display is positive estimated EV, descending, with the best offer per player/prop. Bet-type/market filters and provisional inclusion are available. Single-bet EV does not establish combined-parlay EV.

While the EV view is open, valid pregame quotes and predictions—including negative estimates before display filters—are retained as browser-local research snapshots in IndexedDB after the exact odds commit is verified. Schema 2 also retains the display filters and displayed selection IDs; those fields and the odds commit participate in deduplication. Content-identical records use `add`, preserving the first saved record. Earlier schema-1 records remain intact. **Export saved EV history** downloads these records; **Save EV evidence** downloads the displayed subset. Both include exact market/selection IDs, line, price, capture times, model hashes, history versions, probabilities, availability/lineup context, features, and exclusions. These are editable local research snapshots, not externally verified issuance receipts. Browser storage is not a shared server journal; export a copy for independent retention. Historical PDFs provide outcomes, not earlier offered prices; no historical ROI is reported.

Version **ev-props-v1.1** retains the three official played appearances printed as 00:00, separates raw distribution means from calibrated means, strengthens fixture identity checks, and excludes unsupported quarter-point contracts. The v1 engine, model, protocol and diagnostics are preserved under `audits/2026-10-19-ev/baseline`. The corrected engine was rebuilt using the same chronological windows; the audited archive remains retrospective. Original Parlay ranking logic and its frozen protocol are unchanged.

The supported historical grid has 7,092 Points appearances across 411 fixtures: Brier 0.19038 and log loss 0.56411. Threes has 6,377 appearances across 411 fixtures: Brier 0.18754 and log loss 0.55658. The full grid has lower Brier scores (0.16647 and 0.09079) because it includes a different mix of thresholds, including easier outcomes. These are predefined historical thresholds, not archived FanDuel offers. Comparing the full and supported samples does not establish a filtering benefit. The audit adds calibrated season-mean and recent-form benchmarks, role/sample/line strata, calibration intercept/slope, reliability bins, fixture-cluster intervals and weekly sensitivity. These exploratory comparisons cannot establish future profitable EV.

**Grade saved EV history** downloads a separate, versioned research grading report against verified final boxes in the loaded archive/current-season datasets. It preserves the original forecasts. Exact milestones, half lines, integer pushes and zero-minute played appearances are supported. No participation is a rule-indicated void. Missing PDFs remain pending; inconsistent boxes, incomplete game time, conflicting source revisions and changed fixtures require review. Reports retain the source PDF hash. Revisions produce new reports, never rewrite original snapshots. Illustrative unit returns assume ordinary contract rules; actual sportsbook settlements, venue changes, bonuses and account profit are unverified. Repeated snapshots and alternate lines are correlated and do not constitute an independent performance sample.

The Tennessee basketball rules checked October 19, 2026 include overtime for ordinary player-performance markets and action once a player takes the court. NBA postponement to another scheduled local date, venue changes and suspended-game exceptions require their specific treatment. The public source is [FanDuel Tennessee house rules](https://www.fanduel.com/fanduel-sportsbook-house-rules-tn). The research grader does not silently adjudicate those exceptions or promotional protections. See `EV_FORWARD_PROTOCOL.json` for the prospective evaluation gate; trusted shared pregame receipts remain a prerequisite for verified public track-record claims.
